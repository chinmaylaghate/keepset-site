How each feature works

VoiceOver

Buttons, cards and section headings have clear names. Every entry card offers its actions, like Mark as done, Snooze, Edit, Move, Duplicate and Delete. A two-finger double tap starts or stops a voice moment, and plays or pauses one. Photos and scanned pages are described on your device, and VoiceOver tells you when Undo is available.

Voice Control

Icon buttons have names you can say, like Add, Search, Settings, Notifications, More and Edit. Nothing needs a swipe. A quick tap on the microphone starts a recording that keeps going until you stop it, so you don't have to hold it.

Larger Text

On iPhone and iPad, text grows up to the largest accessibility size. Icons grow with it, and sheets open to full height so there is room to read.

Dark Interface

Every screen has light and dark colors and follows your device setting.

Differentiate Without Color Alone

Keepset never uses color alone to tell you something. "Overdue" is written in words, chips have icons, and calendar markers use different shapes.

Sufficient Contrast

Keepset's colors are chosen to be easy to read in light and dark. They get stronger when you turn on Increase Contrast.

Reduced Motion

With Reduce Motion on, Keepset removes zooming, shrinking, growing and sliding effects.

Captions

Voice moments have written text you can read. If Keepset can't write it, you can try again or type what was said. The Apple Watch doesn't play voice moments or show their text.

Audio Descriptions

Keepset has no video, so there is nothing to describe.
