Support

Keepset is one private place on your iPhone for everything you own: pets, cars, home, people, devices and documents, each with its own timeline.

Get help

Email chinmaylaghate@gmail.com. You can also use Settings, About, Send feedback in the app.

Common questions

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. Keepset has no account and no server. With Keepset Pro you can turn on iCloud sync, which keeps a copy in your own iCloud.

How do I move my data or keep a backup?

Settings, Data, Export all data saves everything in one file. Your iPhone backup also includes Keepset.

How do I restore Keepset Pro?

Settings, Keepset Pro, Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel Keepset Pro?

Settings, Keepset Pro, Manage subscription, or iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions. If Pro ends, nothing is deleted or locked.

Reminders don't show up

Check that notifications for Keepset are on in iOS Settings, Notifications, Keepset.
