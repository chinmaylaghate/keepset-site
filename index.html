Keepset for iPad and Mac.

The same keepers, made for the bigger screen. One app, built for each device, not stretched from the phone.

iPad

Room to see it all.

  • Cards sit two across, so more fits on one screen.
  • Tap a card and its details open beside it, no back and forth.
  • Tabs sit at the top and open into a sidebar.
  • Works in every direction, portrait or landscape.
  • Drop a photo or PDF on a keeper to add it.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: ⌘N new entry, ⇧⌘N new keeper, ⌘F search.

Mac

A real Mac app.

  • A sidebar with every keeper. Pick one and it opens as its own tab.
  • Click a card to see its details on the right.
  • Drag in photos and files from Finder.
  • Menus and shortcuts: ⌘N new entry, ⌘, Settings.
  • Lock Keepset with Touch ID.
  • Next up on your desktop as a widget.
